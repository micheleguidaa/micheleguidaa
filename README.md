## Research highlights

- **[Cognitive Firewall](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01277):** reduced jailbreak attack success to 2% or below on three of four benchmarks while keeping benign over-refusal at 8%.
- **[Agentra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18325):** helped design a supervisable multi-agent LLM system that raised decision F1 from 0.61 to 0.84, with a 0% projected harmful-action rate on a 120-event corpus.
- **[Shortcut learning in early intrusion detection](https://github.com/micheleguidaa/early-nids-artifact):** classified network flows from their first 4 to 5 packets with BiGRU, Transformer, Random Forest, and XGBoost models on four datasets, then showed the scores are inflated by dataset fingerprints: masking header fields cuts macro-F1 by up to 36 points.
- **AI4ESOPP:** built a Maskable PPO scheduler for energy-aware industrial production that achieved zero weighted tardiness across evaluation episodes.
